# Journal fit and preparation notes

Verified 2 October 2026 from official publisher pages.

## Primary target: Frontiers in Aging — Musculoskeletal Aging

Proposed article type: **Hypothesis and Theory**. The [official article-type page](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/aging/for-authors/article-types) permits this format, with a maximum main-text length of 12,000 words and the structure abstract, introduction, relevant subsections and discussion. It is peer reviewed and subject to an article processing charge; the amount and any waiver must be checked at submission. No fee has been paid or submission made.

The [Musculoskeletal Aging scope](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/aging/sections/musculoskeletal-aging/about) includes biological and physiological changes affecting musculoskeletal function and independence. This manuscript's causal question concerns functional healthspan, with clinical survival explicitly deferred. The 2026 [Mathieu et al. paper](https://doi.org/10.3389/fragi.2026.1715723) confirms topical proximity but also creates a substantive overlap that the manuscript acknowledges.

The draft follows the [Frontiers author guidelines](https://www.frontiersin.org/guidelines/author-guidelines): editable Word text and equations, single spacing, continuous line numbers, page numbers, seven keywords, consistent author-date citations, two tables and two original scientific schematics. Bibliography metadata and rendered DOI completeness were checked. Exact portal metadata, citation styling and any editor-specific requirements must be confirmed at submission. The custom CSL produces a consistent author-date draft; it is not represented as an official publisher template.

The central editorial pitch is the sitting pause as a probe separating acute energy recycling from chronic adaptation. Leading with an Okinawan lifespan claim, “quantum” terminology, or an assumed spinal helix would obscure that contribution and exceed the evidence.

## Secondary option: Biogerontology

The [official aims and scope](https://link.springer.com/journal/10522/aims-and-scope) welcome hypotheses concerning aging, but the [submission guidelines](https://link.springer.com/journal/10522/submission-guidelines) do not provide an equally clear standalone Hypothesis and Theory route in the material reviewed. Article-type suitability should be confirmed with the editor before adapting the paper. A review proposal route does not make this a completed review or guarantee acceptance. Frontiers in Aging is the stronger immediate format match.
