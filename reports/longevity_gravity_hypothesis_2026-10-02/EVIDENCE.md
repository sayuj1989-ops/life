# Primary evidence ledger

Accessed 2026-10-02. Focused search, not systematic coverage. Quantitative effects below are reported study results, not estimates produced by this dossier. Indexed primary abstracts/text were used when the direct full-text page was blocked. Search-engine crawl labels were not used as publication dates.

## Search boundary

Queries covered sitting-rising mortality, floor-rise intervention trials, Okinawan floor exposure and demographics, interruption of sitting, mechanosensing, Klotho, FOXO3, meaningful functional change, tendon training in older adults, walking energetics, and rate-dependent tendon measurements. The absence of a directly measured Okinawan floor-rise/survival cohort in these results is an evidence gap. A later systematic search should include Japanese databases and explicit author/data enquiries; none were performed here.

The countercurvature extension added the user's original article, physical information definitions, erasure/feedback thermodynamics, sensory adaptation, and human postural perturbation research. These establish definitions and experimental precedents; the proposed floor-training/control relationship remains a new hypothesis to test.

The 2025 and 2026 pilots constrain enthusiasm: practiced skill, confidence, general mobility, and survival are different outcomes. Retain their null outcomes alongside their favorable findings.
